GAMING VIEWS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Gaming Views ("the app," "we," "us") is a simple release-tracking app for upcoming video games. This policy explains, honestly and completely, what data the app touches.

What we don't do

We don't require an account to use the app. We don't run ads. We don't sell data — we don't have any to sell.

What data the app handles

Your watchlist. The games you save stay on your own device only. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server we operate.

Game release data. The app fetches public game information (titles, release dates, platforms, cover art) from our own backend, which in turn sources it from IGDB (a third-party game database). This is a one-way, anonymous fetch — no personal information about you is sent as part of this request beyond what any web request includes automatically (see "Server logs" below).

Server logs. Like virtually all web services, our backend (hosted on Vercel) automatically logs basic technical information for standard operational and security purposes — such as IP address, timestamp, and which endpoint was called. This is not linked to any personal profile, since we don't have user accounts. These logs are used only for abuse prevention and debugging, and are retained only as long as our hosting provider's standard practice.

Feedback emails. If you use the "Send Feedback" option in Settings, this opens your device's own email app with a message addressed to us. That email is sent directly from your device to our inbox via your own email provider — it does not pass through or get stored by the app or our servers.

Notifications (if enabled). Release-day reminders you set for watchlisted games are scheduled locally on your device. We do not receive any record of what reminders you've set.

Third-party services

  • IGDB / Twitch — source of the public game data shown in the app. No personal data about you is shared with them.
  • Vercel — hosts our backend. Subject to standard server logging as described above.
  • (Future) Steam — if/when real Steam account linking becomes available, this policy will be updated before that feature goes live, and will clearly explain what additional data that involves.

Children's privacy

Gaming Views is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes — for example, when real account linking is added — we'll update this policy before that change ships, not after.
